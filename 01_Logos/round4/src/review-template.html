Making it only yours

Design + legal
Original constructionEvery letter and the mark are drawn from scratch. No font or template is used, so nobody can download the same thing.
Use ™ from day oneAdd ™ next to the logo on every post. It tells people you claim it as your mark.
Register the trademarkFile with IP India (ipindia.gov.in) in Lawrance's name. Class 41 covers sports and events, Class 25 covers clothing.
Then use ®Only after registration is granted. It lets you take action against copies.

No design can be made impossible to copy. Registration is what makes copying illegal and gives you the power to stop it. Before filing, run a trademark search on the IP India site to confirm nothing similar exists.
