# HoopLife Ninja: prompts and steps for the sculpted model

Use **one** of the two routes. Attach `turnaround-sheet.png` wherever a tool or artist accepts reference images.

---

## Route A: AI tools (fast, low cost)

### Step 1: concept image (any image generator that accepts a reference image)
Paste this, attach the turnaround sheet:

> Chibi designer vinyl toy figure of a fierce basketball ninja, full body, front view, standing in a wide stance with fists down and slightly out, on a plain grey studio background, soft studio lighting, sculpted 3D toy render. Black hood up with a centre seam, the back of the hood pulled into a point with a soft floppy tip falling to one side, the brim shading large almond eyes with heavy lids and dark brown irises, sharp V-shaped angry brows, deep brown skin. Black cloth mask over nose, mouth and chin draping onto the chest like a scarf. Short-sleeve black ninja top with a diagonal crossover wrap, bare muscular forearms with wide dark-grey wrist wraps, grey cloth sash knotted at the front with two tails and a thin red line, small pouch on the hip, baggy black ninja pants gathered at the ankles, black lace-up boots. An orange basketball with black seams and a white heartbeat line across the middle at its feet. Original character, no logos, no web pattern.

Make **front, side and back** versions (change "front view" each time). Show them to Lawrance and keep the best set.

### Step 2: image to 3D (Meshy or Tripo: both have image-to-3D)
1. Upload the approved **front** image. Add the side/back images if the tool allows multi-view.
2. Settings: "high quality / PBR textures", "A-pose" if offered, target about 50k triangles.
3. Prompt (if asked): `chibi vinyl toy basketball ninja, pointed floppy hood, cowl mask, crossover ninja top, grey knotted sash, baggy pants, boots`
4. Download as **GLB** (for the web and intro) and **STL** (for printing).

### Step 3: rig and animate (Mixamo, free)
1. Upload the FBX/GLB to mixamo.com and place the auto-rig markers (chin, wrists, elbows, knees, groin).
2. Download these animations "with skin" at 30 fps: *Idle*, *Dribble*, *Jump Shot*, *Victory* (pose), plus any idle you like for the vanish.
3. Send the files back and they'll go into the live figure page, the intro video and the merch mockups.

## Route B: 3D artist (best quality)

Message to post on Fiverr / ArtStation / Behance (attach the brief and the turnaround sheet):

> Hi! I need a **stylised chibi 3D character** for a basketball community brand: a ninja-hooper mascot in a designer-vinyl-toy style. Full brief, colour codes and a 4-view turnaround of a stand-in model are attached.
> **Deliverables:** sculpted model (GLB/FBX, 30–60k tris, 2K PBR textures), swappable black/red mask, Mixamo-compatible rig with extra bones for the hood tip and sash tails, 5 looping animations (stance idle, ball spin, dribble, jump shot, ninja pose + smoke vanish), and a watertight 10 cm print version.
> **Process:** concept/turnaround approval → sculpt approval → rig and animations.
> Please share your portfolio, price and timeline. The character is original, and all rights transfer to us on completion.

Ask for a **commercial-use / full-rights** agreement in writing before paying.
