# HoopLife Ninja: character brief (draft 3, for sculpting)

**What:** the HoopLife Community mascot. Lawrance (@lawrance_hooplife), the community's founder, as a collectible chibi ninja-hooper. It will be used in the intro and outro, stickers, tees, keychains and a printed figurine.
**Live stand-in:** https://claude.ai/artifact/Dv9cDbfnXsfM2was9TiKeb (proportions, outfit, colours, moves).
**Turnaround:** `sculpt-package/turnaround-sheet.png` (front, side, back, ¾).
**Style:** sculpted designer vinyl toy, chibi proportions (head about 40% of the height), soft studio lighting, slightly matte. Fierce but likeable, stocky and athletic, ready to move. **Original character:** the mood reference is for style only. No web pattern, no superhero mask, nothing from an existing character.

## Identity points (must have)
1. **Pointed hood:** black, hood up, a centre seam over the top, the back pulled into a point with a soft floppy tip falling to the figure's left. The brim comes down over the forehead and shadows the eyes.
2. **Cowl mask:** covers nose, mouth and chin, and drapes down onto the chest and shoulders like a scarf, with soft cloth folds. Two versions: **black #101012** and **red #B3121A** (a nod to Lawrance's own red mask).
3. **Eyes:** large almond eyes, heavy upper lids, dark brown irises, a small catch-light. **Brows:** sharp V, determined.
4. **Skin:** deep brown (about #3F2519), visible around the eyes and on the arms and fists.
5. **Stance:** feet wide, fists down and slightly out, chin a little lowered.

## Deliverables
- **Turnaround sheet** (front, side, back, ¾) in A-pose, for Lawrance to approve first.
- **Model:** GLB/FBX, 30–60k triangles, PBR textures (2K), separate meshes for the ball and the mask (to swap black/red).
- **Rig:** humanoid skeleton, Mixamo-compatible, extra bones for the hood tip and the sash tails.
- **Animations** (loops, 30 fps): stance idle, ball spin on finger, crossover dribble, jump shot, ninja signature pose + smoke vanish.
- **Print version:** watertight solid mesh, 10 cm tall, on a round black base with a red rim.

## Approval steps
1. Lawrance approves the turnaround / concept.
2. Lawrance approves the sculpted model.
3. Rig and animations, then the model goes into the intro, website and merch.
